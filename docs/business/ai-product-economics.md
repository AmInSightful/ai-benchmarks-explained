# AI Product Economics

> **AI product economics is the study of how AI capabilities affect product costs, pricing, margins, and business sustainability.**

---

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

After reading this chapter, you will understand:

- Why AI products have different economics from traditional software
- How AI costs are generated
- Key AI financial metrics
- How to design profitable AI products
- How pricing strategies change with AI

---

# Why AI Changes Software Economics

Traditional SaaS:

```
Build Software

↓

Acquire Users

↓

Serve More Users

↓

Low Incremental Cost
```

---

AI products:

```
Build Product

↓

Acquire Users

↓

More Usage

↓

More AI Processing

↓

Higher Variable Cost
```

---

# The Core AI Economics Question

The wrong question:

> "How many users can we acquire?"

---

The better question:

> "Can we create more customer value than the cost of serving each interaction?"

---

# The AI Cost Stack

AI costs usually come from:

```
Model Usage

+

Infrastructure

+

Data

+

Engineering

+

Operations
```

---

# 1. Model Costs

The biggest variable cost for many AI products.

---

Examples:

- API usage
- Token consumption
- Model inference

---

More usage:

↓

More model calls

↓

Higher cost

---

# 2. Infrastructure Costs

Includes:

- Computing resources
- Storage
- Hosting
- Networking

---

Large AI systems require significant infrastructure.

---

# 3. Data Costs

Includes:

- Data collection
- Cleaning
- Storage
- Processing
- Governance

---

Good data can become a competitive advantage.

---

# 4. Engineering Costs

AI products require:

- ML engineers
- Data engineers
- AI infrastructure engineers
- Product teams

---

# 5. Operations Costs

Includes:

- Monitoring
- Evaluation
- Safety systems
- Human review

---

# Understanding Token Economics

AI models process information through tokens.

---

More tokens:

=

More computation

=

Higher cost

---

Token usage depends on:

- Prompt length
- Response length
- Conversation history
- Retrieved documents

---

# Example

Simple chatbot:

Short question

↓

Short answer

↓

Low cost

---

Enterprise assistant:

Large documents

+

Long conversations

↓

Higher cost

---

```

Contribution Margin
```

Examples:

- Cost per chat

- Cost per task completed

---

# Cost Per User

Important for:

# AI Margin

Formula:

```
Revenue

-

AI Gross Margin
```

---

> "Is AI creating more value than it costs?"

---

Positive.

# Pricing AI Products

AI creates new pricing challenges.

---

# Model 1: Subscription Pricing

Example:

$20/month.

---

Advantages:

- Simple
- Predictable revenue

---

Challenge:

Heavy users may become expensive.

---

# Model 2: Usage-Based Pricing

Example:

Pay per:

- Request
- Token
- Task

---

Advantages:

Matches cost.

---

Challenge:

Customers dislike unpredictable bills.

---

# Model 3: Seat-Based Pricing

Example:

$30/user/month.

---

Common in:

Enterprise SaaS.

---

Challenge:

AI usage varies by user.

---

# Model 4: Outcome-Based Pricing

Customer pays for results.

---

Example:

Pay per:

- Resolved support ticket
- Generated report
- Completed task

---

Potential:

Very powerful.

---

Challenge:

Harder to measure.

---

# AI Cost Optimization Strategies

---

# 1. Use the Right Model

Do not use the biggest model for every task.

---

Example:

Simple classification:

Small model.

---

Complex reasoning:

Large model.

---

# 2. Reduce Unnecessary Context

More information:

=

Higher cost.

---

Improve:

- Retrieval
- Summaries
- Context selection

---

# 3. Cache Responses

Reuse previous results.

---

Benefits:

- Lower cost
- Faster responses

---

# 4. Improve Prompts

Better prompts can reduce:

- Tokens
- Errors
- Repeated attempts

---

# 5. Use Human-in-the-Loop Strategically

Not every task requires full automation.

---

Example:

AI creates draft.

Human approves.

---

# Common AI Economics Mistakes

---

# Mistake 1: Free AI Features Without Cost Planning

A popular feature can become expensive.

---

# Mistake 2: Using Expensive Models Everywhere

Not every task needs maximum intelligence.

---

# Mistake 3: Ignoring User Behavior

Heavy users can change economics.

---

# Mistake 4: Pricing Like Traditional SaaS

AI usage creates new cost structures.

---

# Mistake 5: Measuring Only Revenue

A growing AI product can still lose money.

# Key Takeaways

- AI introduces variable costs into software products.
- Successful AI products balance intelligence, cost, and user value.
- Token usage directly impacts economics.
- Pricing models must reflect AI cost structures.
- The best AI products optimize both user value and margins.
