# AI Product Discovery: Finding the Right Problems for AI

> **AI product discovery is the process of identifying valuable user problems where AI can create meaningful improvements in efficiency, experience, decision-making, or automation.**

---

## 🎯 What You'll Learn

After reading this chapter, you will understand:

- Why AI discovery starts with problems, not technology
- How to identify AI opportunities
- How to analyze user workflows
- How to conduct AI-focused user research
- How PMs can validate AI ideas before building

---

# Why AI Discovery Is Different

Traditional product discovery asks:

> "What feature should we build?"

AI discovery asks:

> "Where can intelligence improve the user's workflow?"

---

The goal is not:

```
Add AI
```

The goal is:

```
Improve user outcomes using AI
```

---

# The AI Discovery Mindset

Avoid:

> "Where can we put a chatbot?"

Instead ask:

- Where do users spend too much time?
- Where do users make repeated decisions?
- Where is information difficult to access?
- Where do users need expert knowledge?
- Where do users perform repetitive work?

---

# The AI Opportunity Areas

Most successful AI products solve one or more of these problems:

---

# 1. Information Overload

Users have too much information.

AI helps:

- Summarize
- Search
- Extract insights

---

Examples:

- Document assistants
- Research tools
- Enterprise knowledge systems

---

# 2. Repetitive Work

Users repeat similar tasks.

AI helps:

- Automate
- Generate
- Classify

---

Examples:

- Email drafting
- Report creation
- Ticket classification

---

# 3. Complex Decision Making

Users need help making decisions.

AI helps:

- Analyze information
- Predict outcomes
- Recommend actions

---

Examples:

- Sales forecasting
- Fraud detection
- Medical support systems

---

# 4. Content Creation

Users create information.

AI helps:

- Write
- Design
- Code
- Generate ideas

---

Examples:

- Marketing tools
- Coding assistants
- Design assistants

---

# 5. Personalization

Users have different needs.

AI helps:

- Recommend
- Customize
- Adapt experiences

---

Examples:

- Recommendations
- Learning platforms
- Shopping experiences

---

# Workflow-Based Discovery

A powerful AI discovery technique:

> Analyze the user's workflow, not just their feature requests.

---

Example:

Customer support agent workflow:

```
Receive Ticket

↓

Understand Problem

↓

Search Knowledge Base

↓

Write Response

↓

Update System
```

---

AI opportunities:

Step 1:

Summarize ticket.

Step 2:

Find relevant information.

Step 3:

Draft response.

Step 4:

Update CRM.

---

# Jobs-To-Be-Done for AI

Users do not want AI.

They want outcomes.

---

Weak:

"Users want an AI assistant."

---

Better:

"Users want to answer customer questions faster."

---

The JTBD framework:

```
When ______

I want to ______

So I can ______
```

---

Example:

When:

"I receive complex customer requests"

I want:

"Help understanding the issue"

So I can:

"Resolve requests faster"

---

# AI User Research

Traditional user interviews:

"What features do you want?"

---

AI discovery interviews:

Focus on:

## Friction

"What tasks feel painful?"

---

## Time

"What takes too long?"

---

## Decisions

"What decisions require expertise?"

---

## Repetition

"What do you do frequently?"

---

## Information

"What information is hard to find?"

---

# Questions to Ask Users

Good questions:

---

## Workflow Questions

"Walk me through how you complete this task."

---

## Pain Questions

"What part takes the most effort?"

---

## Decision Questions

"Where do you need help deciding?"

---

## Quality Questions

"What mistakes happen today?"

---

## Automation Questions

"If someone helped you, what would you delegate?"

---

# Finding AI Opportunities From Pain Points

Example:

User says:

"I spend two hours every week creating reports."

---

Discovery:

Problem:

Manual reporting.

---

AI opportunity:

Generate reports automatically.

---

Validation:

Measure:

- Time saved
- Accuracy
- Adoption

---

# AI Opportunity Mapping

Create a table:

| Workflow Step | User Pain | AI Opportunity |
|-|-|-|
| Search documents | Slow | AI retrieval |
| Write reports | Time-consuming | AI generation |
| Analyze data | Complex | AI insights |
| Respond to users | Repetitive | AI assistant |

# The AI Problem Validation Framework

Before building, validate:

---

# Problem Importance

Question:

"Is this painful enough?"

---

# Frequency

Question:

"How often does this happen?"

---

# Current Alternative

Question:

"What do users do today?"

---

# AI Advantage

Question:

"Can AI significantly improve this?"

---

# Success Metric

Question:

"How will we measure improvement?"

---

# AI Discovery Experiments

Do not build everything.

Start with:

---

# Concierge Test

A human provides the AI-like service manually.

Example:

Before building AI support:

Humans create suggested responses.

---

# Prototype Test

Create a lightweight experience.

Example:

Clickable AI assistant prototype.

---

# Wizard-of-Oz Test

Users believe AI is automated while humans operate behind the scenes.

---

# Data Test

Check whether required information exists.

---

# Common AI Discovery Mistakes

---

# Mistake 1: Starting With the Model

Bad:

"We have a powerful model. What should we do?"

---

Better:

"What problem is worth solving?"

---

# Mistake 2: Asking Users for AI Ideas

Users understand problems better than solutions.

---

# Mistake 3: Ignoring Workflow

AI value often comes from improving entire processes.

---

# Mistake 4: Automating Low-Value Tasks

Saving five seconds on a rare task creates little value.

---

# Mistake 5: Skipping Validation

AI prototypes can look impressive but fail in real usage.

---

---

☐ How do users solve it today?

---

## AI Opportunity

---

## Data

---

## Measurement

☐ How will we know it works?

---

# The Mental Model

AI discovery is not:

```
Find places to add AI

↓

Build features
```

It is:

```
Understand workflows

↓

Identify pain points

↓

Find AI advantage

↓

Validate value

↓

Build the right solution
```

---

# Key Takeaways

- Great AI products start with user problems.
- Users describe pain, not AI solutions.
- Workflow analysis is one of the best ways to discover AI opportunities.
- AI is most valuable when it improves repetitive, complex, or information-heavy tasks.
- Validate AI ideas before investing heavily.
